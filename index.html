Ψ-0.6: Adaptive Streams of Thought in a Unified Vision-Language World Model
Khai Loong Aw*, Klemen Kotar*, Baihan Zhang*, Yifan Liu*, Lilian Naing Chen,
Atlas Kazemian, Rahul Mysore Venkatesh, Daniel L. K. Yamins
* denotes equal contribution
arXiv, 2026 (under review at ICLR 2027)
project page / arXiv / code

Chain-of-thought transformed AI reasoning, but it is limited to words. What's the physical-world analogue? We introduce Ψ-0.6, one model that reasons in streams of thought: words, depth, motion, camera pose, and imagined scenes – and learns which to use for each problem. Ψ-0.6 surpasses leading open-weight models zero-shot on challenging spatial reasoning and makes two breakthroughs. First, it thinks beyond words, in streams of thought. Second, it learns how to think: which representation to use, and when.
